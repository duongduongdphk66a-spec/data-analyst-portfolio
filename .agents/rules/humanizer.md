# Humanizer Rule

When writing or rewriting documentation, markdown files, UI text, or commit messages:

- Eliminate AI tells: "not X but Y", "let's dive in", "let that sink in", forced triads, and em dashes.
- Eliminate filler buzzwords: *delve, testament, tapestry, landscape, pivotal, robust, showcasing, underscores*.
- Use direct verbs: replace *serves as, boasts, features* with *is, has, includes*.
- Avoid repetitive bold formatting on every list bullet or decorative emojis on headings.
- Maintain accuracy of technical details, numbers, dates, code, and paths.
